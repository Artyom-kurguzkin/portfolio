# Visual Style Specification

## Purpose
A calm, hand-made look: flat paper cards on a dark doodled background, in a small palette.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Palette
Accent colours SHALL be blue, green and yellow only. Orange SHALL NOT be used.

### Requirement: Yellow means interactive
Interactive elements SHALL use the yellow accent with a dark outline, matching the hand-drawn
yellow cursor: back buttons, the gallery arrow, zoom badges, the project link button and
keyboard focus outlines.

### Requirement: Flat cards, single border
Content SHALL sit in flat cards (`.panel`): a plain fill, light paper grain and one solid line
inside the edge. Cards SHALL NOT be nested in other bordered containers. Jagged or sketchy lines
SHALL NOT be used; hover and selected outlines are dashed, with long dashes and wide gaps.

### Requirement: Hand-drawn cursors
Over a card the cursor SHALL be the hand-drawn yellow arrow, and over anything clickable the
matching pointing hand.
